\begin{abstract}
Two low-rank adapters fine-tuned on disjoint domains (Spring Festival vs.\ summer) are
shown to encode \emph{separable concept spaces}: their weight updates $\Delta W$ are
near-orthogonal (global overlap $-0.002$ to $0.135$ across three seeds) while their input
(read) subspaces are indistinguishable (all pairwise top-$k$ similarities
$\approx 3.8$, flat). A data-mixture ``intermediate'' adapter is an \emph{interpolation},
not a third space (registered null model wins). Behavioural (lexical) separation is
strong (hit rate 0.84 vs 0.11), yet output distributions of the two specialists are
\emph{closer to each other than either is to the base} at every position of an answer,
on content and style tokens alike --- composability is therefore a property of the
weight geometry plus the gate, not of output distinguishability. A learned gate
(0.90 accuracy; routed hit 0.800) beats a lexical router (0.80; 0.607), and the composed
runtime $W=W_0+\sum_i p_i\,\Delta W_i$ ships as 374--392\,MB GGUF at 150--166 tokens/s on
an M1 laptop. Cross-domain replication over five domains (adding code, legal, medical)
keeps every specialist pair within $|\Delta W\text{ov}|<0.13$, with the interpolation
anchor ($0.62$/$0.66$) proving the metric detects non-orthogonality when it exists.
All numbers are pinned, four-way hash-verified, and reproducible from committed scripts;
one self-audited sampling bug and its corrected rerun are reported in full.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup}
Base: Qwen2.5-0.5B (frozen, manifest-verified bytes). Adapters: LoRA $r{=}16$, 7 target
modules, lr $10^{-4}$, 6 epochs, seeds 13/14/15; corpora of 55 (spring, summer), 110
(mixture), 40 (code/legal/medical, deviation registered) hand-written Chinese QA pairs.
Probes: 40 questions (15/15/10), hashed \emph{before} corpus authoring; 6-gram leakage
tripwire = 0. Pre-registration precedes all runs (PREREG.md + four addenda); negative
results are reported as first-class. Venue: \texttt{chora/experiments/exp10-concept-adapters}.

\section{Method summary}
\textbf{Behaviour (10a):} 5 sampled generations/probe; lexical hit rates; top-50
answer-start distributions, JS over union support.
\textbf{Geometry (10b/10f):} per-module SVD of $A\in\mathbb{R}^{r\times d_{in}}$,
$B\in\mathbb{R}^{d_{out}\times r}$; read-similarity $\|V_i V_j^{\!\top}\|_F$ at
90\%-energy $k$, write-similarity $\|U_i^{\!\top}U_j\|_F$; basis-invariant global
$\langle\Delta W_i,\Delta W_j\rangle/(\|\Delta W_i\|\|\Delta W_j\|)$; random-flat null
$k/\sqrt d$; invasion = spectral band of the base weights the adapters write into.
\textbf{Sequence (10c):} teacher-forced per-position JS on greedy reference texts;
content-vs-style token classification under two definitions (function-word-inclusive,
and punctuation-only).
\textbf{Systems (10d/10g/GGUF):} multi-adapter runtime with lexical router, then logistic
gate on frozen-base sentence embeddings; merge, convert to GGUF f16, quantize q4\_K\_M,
llama-bench tg128.

\section{Self-audit}
The first router run reseeded \emph{inside} the sampling loop: five ``independent'' draws
were identical (reported 0.70 was a single-draw number in five costumes). Disclosed the
same day, fixed, rerun (v2: 0.607); v1 bytes remain pinned and immutable. Two further
first-run bugs (temperature polarity, negative concentration floor) were exposed by the
companion park-side breathing experiment (C3) and fixed in both artefacts the same day.

\section{Reading}
Concept separation is where the adapter \emph{writes}; what it \emph{reads} is the shared
commonsense of the base. $W_0+\sum_i p_i\Delta W_i$ is therefore feasible as a
composition law (near-orthogonality measured, five domains, one base size) \emph{and}
needs the gate: outputs alone do not reveal the concepts (10c), so the $\sum$ must be
carried by a learned selector (10g). A time-modulated prior pointer is the selector's
natural extension --- previewed symbolically in the sister park the same day.

\section{Limits and next}
One 0.5B base, toy corpora, one gate class; magnitudes not production claims. Exp11:
5 bases $\times$ 4 domains + universal-LoRA probe; MLP/neural controller; perplexity
accounting for merged bf16.
