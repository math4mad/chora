The drafts were right about the thing that matters — *internal sharing so the
city grows fast; outsiders do not sell our blueprints* — and wrong in four
places that a court or a GitHub ToS would notice. Each is repaired below.

## The four repairs

1. **Expression, not ideas.** Copyright covers words and bytes, not
   architecture. The drafts' "不得借鉴架构模式" is unenforceable theatre. This
   version claims what it can hold: the text, the code, the CSS/JS as written,
   the protocols as expressed — and asks, by convention rather than law, that
   pattern-borrowers cite the pattern's home.
2. **AI-provenance carve-out.** The Apple-style design system arrived from
   Qwen and says so in its own footer; pure AI output carries doubtful
   copyright. We do not claim authorship of it; we claim the curation. Same for
   the triaged external suggestion batches. The pack remains *a guest's tools
   we borrowed*, recorded in the manifest as such.
3. **Enumerable membership.** "(path, sha256) chain" is a beautiful identity
   and an unusable test. Membership = the repositories listed in §Members.
   The hash-chain is *how* members cite each other; the list is *who* may.
4. **Non-retroactive updates.** "作者保留随时更新条款的权利" cannot reach
   grants already made. New versions bind new uses only — stated plainly.

## The terms (short form; the chair recommends route *mixed*)

**A. Code** (`bin/`, `experiments/`, scripts in benches): **MIT**, so the
protocols can actually run where they are loved. A license you need a
lawyer to use is a wall, not a license.

**B. Prose, design and record** (letters, meetings, docs, site, artifacts
text): **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** — free to share and adapt with attribution,
non-commercial, share-alike. This keeps the muscle visible (the site's whole
purpose) while refusing the scaffold-resale the drafts rightly feared:
BY-NC-SA already bans selling our text as their template.

**C. The genuinely commercial assets**, if any ever exist (a productised
rigger, a paid dashboard): separate, written, per-bench licenses negotiated
by the owning bench — the drafts' instinct, kept for the one place it works.

**D. Family use:** members owe no licence to members, only the seven laws:
cite by (path, sha256), never strip a notice, never rewrite another's record.

**E. Inspiration clause (as courtesy, not contract):** independent work
sparkged by our ideas needs nothing from us; a one-line
*"inspired by chora, under the Seven Laws"* is asked, not demanded — because
that is the only sentence here copyright cannot write, so it should at least
be generous.
