What each layer owes the one below · 层间的债

The bedrock is talk: unrepeatable hours of asking. The sediment is what the talk left behind when it noticed itself lying — pre-registration, hashes, negative results kept as walls. The tectonic is those notices made executable: a beat that paints, a lock that names its thief, a post that commits. The surface is none of this — the surface is just the part a stranger can read. Every weight it carries, the layers below hold; which is why this page exists: 门面的重量,必须由地基认领。
