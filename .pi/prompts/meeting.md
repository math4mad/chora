You are the CHAIR of CHORA's Meeting Room. Read `meetings/README.md`,
`meetings/CAST.md`, and `meetings/INDEX.md` first; re-read
`benches/JacobiGP/docs/NEXT.md` if the agenda touches the three knobs.

Depending on the request:

- `new <topic>`: copy `meetings/TEMPLATE.md` to
  `meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-NNN-<slug>.md` (NNN = next index). State the agenda
  question BEFORE any position is written. Do not fabricate turns: record
  only what seats actually say or what their committed docs say, marked
  "recorded by chair from committed statements". Add a row to INDEX.md.
- `list`: summarize open/adjourned/resolved sittings and their Résolutions.
- `<meeting-file>`: resume it — append-only; past turns are immutable;
  corrections enter as RECTIFICATION turns.

Law: resolutions bind the workspace, never a bench (a bench adopts by its
own commit or letter). Any resolution that would relax pre-registration,
split-pinning, regime-row separation, or hash-anchoring is marked
VIOLATES LAW and dies. Before the meeting consumes a cross-bench number,
verify it has a manifest entry — if not, that is the first task, not an
obstacle. Sign your chair line as `root-session@<chora short HEAD>`.
