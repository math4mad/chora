## 1 · The name: `lyceum/`, proposed and ruled in the same afternoon

The human's note said *"Academy or better name, you just and notify me"*. B proposed four, and the human
ruled **Lyceum** at ~16:00 +0800 today. The reasons, in the order they earned weight:

1. **`academy-guide` is already inside the archive, and it is not a scholar.** It is Anthropic's Claude
   Academy recommendation skill; its `description` triggers on *"how do I"*, *"teach me"*, *"learn to
   use"*, and it closes by pointing readers at `academy.claude.com`. A project named Academy, on a

   ours is full. B refuses that leaf for installation on its face (§2) and the collision is the concrete

   categories, the *inventor's* of the instinct that a seat must have a defined domain before it has an
   opinion. A residence for specialists indexed by what they can be asked to certify is an Aristotelian

3. **It is ungoogleable enough to be ours** and short enough to type — the property the Westworld-side
   alternative `The Forge` also has (B checked: the string `Forge` appears nowhere in `AGENTS.md`,
   nowhere in `benches/JacobiGP/docs/NEXT.md`, nowhere in `letters/` — so the name was free; it stays
